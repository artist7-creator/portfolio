Getting Started

Install Pavlet EQ from the App Store and grant access to your music library when prompted. Pick a track to start playback, then open the Equalizer to shape your sound. Save your favorite settings as a profile so you can recall them instantly.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Pavlet EQ plays your local music library on iPhone.
  • Equalization is applied in real time, on-device.
  • You can save unlimited custom EQ profiles.
  • Background playback is supported.

Troubleshooting

If the app behaves unexpectedly, try these steps first:

  • Make sure you're running the latest version of Pavlet EQ and iOS.
  • Force-quit and reopen the app.
  • Confirm Pavlet EQ has permission to access your music library in Settings.
  • Restart your device if issues persist.

Playback Issues

No sound or stuttering playback?

  • Check that your device isn't muted and the volume is up.
  • Disconnect and reconnect Bluetooth headphones if audio routes incorrectly.
  • Verify the file is a supported, non-DRM audio format stored on device.
  • Disable the EQ temporarily to confirm it isn't a profile setting.

EQ Questions

Getting the most from the parametric equalizer:

  • Each band has frequency, gain, and Q controls.
  • Use narrow Q to target specific frequencies, wide Q for broad tone shaping.
  • Small adjustments often sound best — start with a few dB.
  • Save a profile once you're happy with the result.

Import Issues

Trouble importing an EQ configuration?

  • Confirm the configuration is in a supported format.
  • Make sure the file isn't corrupted or empty.
  • Try importing again after restarting the app.
  • Still stuck? Email us the file and we'll take a look.

App Updates

We regularly improve Pavlet EQ with new features and fixes. Keep automatic updates enabled in the App Store, or update manually from your App Store account page. Your saved EQ profiles are preserved across updates.
