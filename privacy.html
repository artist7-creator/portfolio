Privacy Policy

Pavlet EQ is built to respect your privacy. This policy explains what information the app does and does not handle.

Overview

Pavlet EQ respects user privacy. The app is a music player that runs on your iPhone and is designed to work without collecting unnecessary personal information. We only handle the minimum data required to run the app and, where you opt in, to improve it.

Your audio files

Your music stays yours. Audio files remain on your device and are played locally. Pavlet EQ does not upload, copy, or share your audio files unless you explicitly choose to share them using the standard iOS sharing features. Equalization and playback are processed on-device.

Personal information

Pavlet EQ does not collect unnecessary personal information. We do not require you to create an account to use the app, and we do not sell your data. Any information you choose to send us directly — for example, when you email support — is used only to respond to your request.

Crash diagnostics

If you enable diagnostics, Pavlet EQ may collect crash and performance information to help us identify and fix problems. This data is technical in nature (such as device model, operating system version, and the state of the app at the time of a crash) and is used solely to improve the stability of the app. You can control diagnostics sharing in your device settings.

Analytics

Pavlet EQ may use anonymous analytics to understand how features are used and to improve the app. Where analytics are used, the information is aggregated and not used to identify you personally. We aim to keep any such collection minimal and privacy-respecting.

Children's privacy

Pavlet EQ is not directed to children under the age required by your local laws, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Pavlet EQ after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact us

If you have any privacy questions or requests, you can contact us at ompatilwork07@gmail.com. We're happy to help.
